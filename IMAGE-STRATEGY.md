# Namma Rang Painters — image and photography plan

## Launch-stage rule
Namma Rang has not yet supplied completed-project evidence. Do not publish stock images as completed work, do not create fake before/after pairs, and do not claim customer permission or testimonials that have not been obtained.

## Images to capture first
1. Sunny and Manoj: natural, well-lit owner portraits.
2. Own painting team: team photograph, only if the pictured workers are actually part of the team.
3. Tools and materials: real rollers, brushes, masking tape, drop sheets, ladders and product tins.
4. Process photographs: surface cleaning, crack filling, sanding, masking, priming and paint application, but only for work actually offered.
5. Colour consultation: genuine shade cards or sample boards.

## Every real project
- Obtain the customer's permission before taking or publishing photographs.
- Capture a wide "before" image from a fixed position.
- Capture preparation and application stages.
- Capture the completed surface from the same position and in comparable lighting.
- Do not show house numbers, faces, family photos, vehicle registration numbers or private documents without explicit permission.
- Keep the original full-resolution copy; publish compressed WebP copies for the website.

## Naming and alt text
Use descriptive, truthful filenames:
- namma-rang-interior-painting-bengaluru-project-01.webp
- wall-preparation-house-painting-hsr-layout.webp
- exterior-house-painting-electronic-city.webp

Alt text should describe the actual image, not stuff keywords:
"Painter applying interior wall paint in a Bengaluru apartment."
Do not put locality names in alt text unless the photograph is actually from that locality.

## Image dimensions / performance
- Hero: landscape 1600 x 900 WebP, target under 250 KB where practical.
- Gallery: 1200 x 900 WebP, target under 180 KB where practical.
- Team portraits: 800 x 1000 WebP, target under 150 KB where practical.
- Use responsive images and lazy-load below-the-fold gallery images.
- Keep descriptive captions where they add useful context.
