    Questions

    Good to know.

    How long does it take to paint a 2BHK?

    A standard repaint of a 2BHK apartment takes about 3 to 4 working days. Fresh putty work or textures can add 1 to 2 days.

    Do I need to move my furniture out?

    No. Our crew moves furniture to the centre of each room and covers it with plastic sheets. Floors, switches and fittings are masked as well.

    Can you paint during the monsoon?

    Interiors, yes, any time of year. For exteriors we plan around the rain and use fast-drying primers. Waterproofing is best done before the monsoon (March to May).

    How does payment work?

    We take 30% upfront for materials and the rest after handover. UPI, bank transfer and cards are all accepted, and you get a GST invoice.

    Do you work in gated communities and apartments?

    Yes. We regularly work in Prestige, Sobha, Brigade and other gated societies, and we follow association rules on working hours and lift use.
