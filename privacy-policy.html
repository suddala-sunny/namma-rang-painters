Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this website handles and how. Last updated: 2 November 2026.

Who we are

Namma Rang Painters is a house-painting service operating online in Bengaluru, Karnataka. The website is run by Suddala Sunny and Manoj Kumar. If you have any privacy question, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

What this website does not do

  • It has no user accounts, logins or payment pages.
  • The enquiry form does not send your details to a server owned by us. Nothing you type is stored by this website.

When you send an enquiry

If you fill in the enquiry form and press the button, the website prepares a WhatsApp message containing the name, phone number, locality, service and notes you entered, and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to Sunny or Manoj. The message is only sent when you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and is subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

If you call or message us directly, we will see your phone number and whatever you choose to share. We use it only to respond to your enquiry, discuss your painting requirements and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. We do not sell your information. Please do not send sensitive information such as bank details or identity documents in an enquiry.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used, such as which pages are visited and how many people click the WhatsApp or phone buttons. When enabled, Google receives technical information from your browser such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and approximate location, and may set cookies or similar identifiers. We use this information in aggregate to improve the website. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or use a browser extension that blocks Google Analytics. Google's practices are described in its own privacy policy.

Fonts and other third-party services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google servers and your IP address is visible to Google when a page loads. Links to WhatsApp (wa.me) take you to services run by Meta.

Photographs of our work

We will publish photographs of completed projects only with the customer's permission.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry messages and customer details only for as long as needed to respond, carry out the work and handle any follow-up or warranty discussion, unless the law requires us to keep records longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to any records we are legally required to keep.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
