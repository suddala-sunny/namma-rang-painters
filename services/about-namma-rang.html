About Namma Rang Painters

Namma Rang Painters is building a customer-focused painting service for homes across Bengaluru. Customers can speak directly with Suddala Sunny or Manoj Kumar to discuss the property, required finish and quotation options.

WhatsApp SunnyRequest an estimate

Direct owner communication

Sunny and Manoj are the listed business contacts. Customers can choose whom to contact by phone or WhatsApp through the website enquiry form.

Our service approach

We aim to make the scope clear before work begins: identify the surfaces, discuss preparation, agree on paint options, record inclusions and exclusions, and confirm the schedule in writing.

Service coverage

We intend to serve customers across Bengaluru, including Electronic City, HSR Layout, BTM Layout, Sarjapur Road, Whitefield, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Varthur, Koramangala, JP Nagar, Jayanagar, Indiranagar, Hebbal and Yelahanka. Availability is confirmed case by case.

Online operations

Namma Rang currently operates online and does not publish a customer-facing office address. Site visits, where agreed, are arranged at the customer's property.
