Interior painting for flats and homes

Interior painting can include bedrooms, living rooms, dining areas, kitchens, passages and ceilings. The work scope should state which surfaces are included, the number of coats, paint range and any preparation required.

What affects the quotation?

  • Paintable wall and ceiling area
  • Existing paint condition and surface repairs
  • Primer, putty and sanding requirements
  • Paint brand and finish selected
  • Furniture movement, masking and access

Labour-only or material plus labour

Ask for either a labour-only quotation or a material-and-labour package. Compare the written scope, product names, number of coats and exclusions before confirming.

How to prepare for an estimate

Share your locality, approximate carpet area, number of rooms and photographs of the walls. A site assessment may be needed to confirm paintable area and preparation work.

Discuss your painting requirements

Share your locality, property type, approximate area and a few current wall photographs. The team can discuss the next steps and whether a site assessment is needed.
