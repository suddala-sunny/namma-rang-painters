House Painting Cost in Bangalore: What Should You Budget?

Painting cost depends on paintable area, surface condition, paint range, preparation, number of coats and access. Namma Rang Painters offers labour-only and material-plus-labour estimate options. Use the website calculator as an initial guide, not a final quote.

What determines painting cost?

  • Actual paintable wall and ceiling area, not carpet area alone
  • New painting versus repainting
  • Putty, crack filling, sanding and primer
  • Paint brand, product range and finish
  • Number of coats and colours
  • Scaffolding, height and site access

Labour-only versus material plus labour

Labour-only estimates exclude paint and consumables unless the quotation explicitly states otherwise. Material-plus-labour estimates should name the paint products, primer, number of coats and preparation scope.

1BHK, 2BHK and 3BHK estimates

Bedroom count is only a rough starting point. Two flats with the same BHK configuration can have different carpet areas, ceiling heights, layouts and wall conditions. Request a site-based quotation for a reliable figure.

Questions to ask before accepting a quote

  1. Which rooms and surfaces are included?
  2. What preparation and number of coats are included?
  3. Which paint products and finishes will be used?
  4. What work is excluded and how are extras approved?
  5. What is the schedule and payment milestone?

Discuss your painting requirements

Share your locality, property type, approximate area and a few current wall photographs. The team can discuss the next steps and whether a site assessment is needed.
