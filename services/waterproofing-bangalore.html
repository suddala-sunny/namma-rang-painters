Waterproofing Services in Bangalore

Damp patches, peeling paint and water seepage need more than a fresh coat of paint. Namma Rang Painters can discuss the visible problem and the need for a site assessment. The correct solution depends on identifying the source of moisture.

WhatsApp SunnyRequest an estimate

Identify the source first

Moisture may come from plumbing leaks, terrace exposure, external cracks, bathroom leakage or rising damp. A surface photograph alone cannot confirm the cause.

Information to share

  • Where the damp patch appears
  • When it becomes visible or worsens
  • Whether it is near a bathroom, terrace or external wall
  • Photographs from inside and outside, if safely accessible

Waterproofing and repainting are different scopes

Painting over a damp surface without resolving the moisture source can lead to recurring problems. The assessment should explain the proposed treatment, preparation, product system and exclusions.

Request an assessment

Send your locality and photographs to start a discussion. Do not access roofs, ledges or unsafe areas to take photographs.

Discuss your painting requirements

Share your locality, property type, approximate area and a few current wall photographs. The team can discuss the next steps and whether a site assessment is needed.
